## What is Artifical Intelligence ?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the capability of computational systems (machines) to perform tasks typically associated with human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making. For example, playing chess, driving cars, generating images, recommending movies etc. This is very new concept (around 80 years back) as compared to other technologies, and it is evolving very fast.

### Evolution of AI

![](/assets/2026-10-24-13-41-00.png)

- In 1950, Alan Turing proposed the Turing Test, a method to determine whether a machine can exhibit intelligent behavior indistinguishable from that of a human. This marked the beginning of AI research. He was trying to answer a question: **"Can machines think ?"** Interestingly, the AI term was not even coined at the time, but coined later in 1955.

![](/assets/2026-10-20-14-07-42.png)

- In 1955, John McCarthy coined the term "Artificial Intelligence". In just 70 years, today in 2025, AI has become a part of our daily lives. They are making very BOLD claims at the time when there were no GPUs, no cloud computing, no big data etc.

> [!NOTE]
> Researches came together with an ambitious belief: _**Every aspect of learning or intelligence could, in principle, be described so precisely that a machine could be made to simulate it.**_

![](/assets/2026-10-24-13-26-22.png)

- After above, there was first AI winter, where there was a lot of hype and promises, but not much progress. The funding dried up, and many researchers left the field. The main reason for this was the lack of computational power and data. But in 1986, a term "Synthetic Intelligence" was coined. Researches were divided into two groups, one group was working on one form of intelligence, and the other group was trying different form of intelligence. Researchers were even debating over the term "Artificial Intelligence" and "Synthetic Intelligence".

| Artitical Intelligence                                                                 | Synthetic Intelligence                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The capability of machines to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. | The capability of machines to learn and adapt to new situations, and learn themselves. |
| Focuses on simulating human intelligence and behavior.                                 | Focuses on enabling machines to learn and improve their performance over time.         |
| Emphasizes reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.                            | Emphasizes learning from data and experience.                                          |

> [!TIP]
> In today's LLM models are categorized as Artitical Intelligence, and Synthetic is still theoretical. But, in future, we may see a shift towards Synthetic Intelligence as machines become more capable of learning and adapting on their own. LLMs are also going beyond human intelligence.

- In 1997, IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov (The greatest player of chess), marking a significant milestone in AI history. This event demonstrated the potential of AI to outperform humans in specific tasks, and raised a question "Have machines become more intelligent than humans ?" in news and articles. But, deep blue was a very specific AI, which was trained to play chess based on permutations and combinations (mathematics) of chess moves. It was not a machine which could think like humans.

> [!NOTE]
> Between 1950 and 1980, Intelligence was just a simple collection of rules `(Rule Based AI)`. For example, if a spam detector:
>
> - if the content contains the word "free", "$$$", "lottery", then mark it as spam.
> - if the content contains the word "hello", "hi", "greetings", then mark it as not spam.
>
> These rules were defined by human experts. These machines were called "Expert Systems". Then, second AI winter came.
